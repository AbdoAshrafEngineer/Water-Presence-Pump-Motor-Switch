### Water-Presence Pump Motor Switch Test (Low Power)

[![Water-Presence Pump Motor Switch Test (Low Power)](http://img.youtube.com/vi/26/0.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ij6da2kLpiU "Water-Presence Pump Motor Switch Test (Low Power)")

This video demonstrates a water-presence pump motor switch being tested with low power loads. The presenter dips wires into a glass of water, which activates a connected lightbulb. The lightbulb turns off when the wires are removed from the water. The presenter repeats this process a few times, showing the light turning on and off as the wires are dipped in and out of the water.
